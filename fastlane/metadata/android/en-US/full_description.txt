Hadith Searcher is a free and open-source application for searching prophetic hadiths, accessing their explanations, sources, and the rulings of hadith scholars. Based on the content of the Al-Durar Al-Sunniyyah website, it gathers hadith information in one place to facilitate reading and study.
Features:

- Search - Search by any word, all words, or exact phrase, with the ability to select a search range and exclude words or phrases.

- Hadith Data - Displays the narrator, source, hadith scholar, summary of the hadith scholar's ruling, hadith classification, and its origins.
- Explanations - Access explanations and interpretations of hadiths.
- Similar Hadiths - Search for hadiths similar to the displayed hadith.
- Advanced Filtering - Select from thousands of options based on the hadith's grade, hadith scholars, books, or narrators.
- Saved - Save hadiths for offline reading.
- Appearance Customization - Control the font size and type of hadiths and support various application colors.
